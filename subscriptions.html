Subscriptions & Refunds

Updated October 19, 2026

Plans

Annual

$2.90 / year

Auto-renews; cancel anytime.

Lifetime

$9.90 once

One purchase, permanent access.

Trial

The first use includes a 7-day free trial. It does not automatically charge; continuing after expiry requires purchasing Annual or Lifetime.

Auto-renewal

The annual subscription charges through your Apple Account and renews within 24 hours before the period ends unless canceled.

Cancel

Open Apple Account → Subscriptions → Pixora Pro Annual → Cancel Subscription.

Restore

In the app: Settings → Pixora Pro → Restore Purchases. Offer codes also use Apple’s official flow.

Refunds

Apple processes subscription payments and refunds. For help, email me.wangjun@qq.com.
