Help Center

Answers for organizing, privacy, purchases, and restore.

Gestures

Swipe left for previous and right for next. Swipe up for Trash and down for Later. Right-browsed photos do not repeat in the next automatic batch; only Later returns in the next session.

Is deletion safe?

Yes. Swiping up moves a photo to Pixora Trash. You can restore it there; final cleanup also requires the iOS system confirmation.

Photo permissions

Pixora needs Photos access to read photos, metadata, and album structure. iOS lets you choose full or limited access; unauthorized photos are never read.

Restore purchases

Go to Settings → Pixora Pro and tap Restore Purchases. Sign in with the Apple Account used to purchase. Annual and Lifetime both support restore.

Cancel a subscription

Open Apple Account → Subscriptions, choose Pixora Pro Annual, then cancel. Access remains until the current period ends.
