Privacy Policy

Updated October 19, 2026

Summary

Pixora reads and processes photos on your device. We do not upload your photos, provide a server account, or sell personal information.

Data we read

  • Photo and video objects, pixel size, creation date, media type, and file size.
  • Location, album membership, screenshot/Live Photo/HDR, and related metadata for on-device suggestions.
  • Favorites, archives, Later, and Trash state resulting from your choices.

Permissions

Photos access is used only for organizing. Notification permission is requested only when reminders are enabled. Location metadata comes from Photos; we do not request continuous location tracking.

Where processing happens

Organizing, similarity analysis, blur analysis, and recommendations run on device. System diagnostics, if collected, follow Apple defaults and do not include original photos.

Purchases

Apple processes payment, subscriptions, and offer codes. We never receive full payment details.

Retention and deletion

Organizing records stay local. Deleting the app or clearing system storage removes local records; favorites/album changes already applied to the system photo library follow system behavior.

Children

Pixora is not directed to children under 13.
